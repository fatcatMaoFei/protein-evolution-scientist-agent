# GFP Predictor V2 and Scientific Agent V2

Frozen before scoring any V2 held-out labels or querying any V2 acquisition pool. Baseline `23adae08bb4929af2c82490ad010c5aa4e263200`. The 450 formal and 540 supplemental V1 queries, V1 model outputs and audits, API/CSV workflow, and V1 report are archival. The scope is isolated *Aequorea victoria* GFP fluorescence in the same Sarkisyan assay. The experiment replays published measurements.

## Partition and prediction

- The V2 manifest uses genotype IDs only. It conservatively retires every canonical GFP genotype or substitution token found in V1 GFP evidence at the baseline Git commit. The remaining sorted IDs are shuffled once with seed 49730217. Blocks are non-overlapping across all roles except the common training-only WT reference. Splits and block IDs are held fixed. The independent units are disjoint archive genotype blocks, not independent assays or experimental laboratories.
- Development blocks 2001–2004 and test blocks 2011–2014 each have nested 96 and 2,048 observation budgets including WT, plus 512 distinct validation or test genotypes. No genotype from these blocks can enter either acquisition pool. Archive `DMS_score` is log10 fluorescence, higher is better, WT 3.7192121319. Thresholds depend on the measured WT or the predeclared top-decile evaluation rule, never on a model's prediction.
- Fixed models in precedence order: Ridge, RF, physicochemical Ridge, pairwise Ridge, histogram boosting, hurdle, and the fixed 50/50 pairwise Ridge–hurdle blend. The hurdle models fluorescence class at 2.5 and conditional outcomes. Use mean validation average precision for specifically discovering above-WT candidates, then Spearman, upper-decile AP, and the listed precedence to resolve ties. Save the selected overall model, best baseline and best enhancement per budget before test scoring. Do not revise choices after test outcomes. Report all prespecified model metrics and both data regimes. Compare AP, Pearson/Spearman, MSE/MAE, top-5/10 precision and discovery, and error-risk usefulness. Empirical uncertainty coverage and risk-error correlation characterize heuristics rather than a calibrated scientific interval.

## Acquisition

- Conventional blocks 2101–2108 and agent blocks 2201–2208 each have 96 initial observations including WT and 96 queryable genotypes. Every policy starts from the same block-specific information. Each decision selects five eligible IDs; three rounds without replacement give 15 true archive queries per arm. Eligibility rules, query budget and data access are identical within each block. Every arm operates on its own observations and fitted model. Read truth for selected IDs only after recording the decision. Primary endpoint is the best newly measured score among the 15 queries. Secondary endpoints are the mean new measurement, above-WT hits, per-round batch outcomes, and gain over starting best. Include every frozen block, even when no potential improvement exists. Record completed and incomplete trajectories explicitly.
- Conventional arms are Random, frozen RF, frozen Ridge and the low-budget validation-selected enhancement (or the overall selected model if already enhanced). Independent agent arms are A uniform Random, B greedy selected low-budget predictor, C rank by estimated fluorescence plus 0.5 times the same model's risk heuristic, D the identical predictor with DeepSeek agent and knowledge off, and E the identical agent with knowledge on. Ties in model ranking break by genotype ID. All arms have access to the same full eligible feature-only pool and may query any five IDs therein. D/E use one OpenAI-compatible DeepSeek V4.1 Flash profile with fixed decoding parameters recorded per call. E adds only auditable sequence chemistry and observation summaries, never hidden measurements. Record served IDs, tool outputs, requests/responses, failures and schema-repair or rate-limit retries. No provider fallback or seed substitution.
- Primary agent contrasts compare D and E against the better realized non-LLM policy of B/C within each block. Also show D/E minus B and C separately and E minus D. Report matched point differences and descriptive 95% paired bootstrap percentile intervals on the block axis, including counts of positive, negative and tied effects. With eight blocks these provide uncertainty context, rather than claims about all possible GFP assays. C is a deterministic acquisition comparator. Its risk score is a model-specific heuristic, not demonstrated posterior uncertainty.
- Sensitivity check before execution: with eight blocks, a paired standard deviation of 0.08 log10 units gives a two-sided t-interval half-width of about 0.067, so contrasts near 0.02 cannot generally be resolved at that variability. The endpoint remains the best newly measured candidate to avoid saturating on the initial incumbent. Treat weak or inconsistent signals as inconclusive and preserve all paired outcomes.
- The closed experiment measures ranking and decision value over a common measured pool. A separate API/CSV example allows new variants outside that roster. Without imported or explicitly replayed measurements those variants are prediction-only and excluded from the verified acquisition contrasts. Hypotheses must identify the selected target, relevant comparator (WT or current incumbent), direction and precommitted evidence. Threshold passage establishes the stated empirical comparison. Unsupported biochemical mechanisms are neither truth nor an acquisition outcome.

## Feedback audit and delivery gates

- Fix the two earliest agent blocks (2201–2202), decision round 2, and both knowledge conditions. With model inputs, candidate roster, scores, and chronological past held constant, compare true newly revealed measurements against an omitted-feedback counterfactual. Duplicate identical inputs to quantify response instability. Reveal the archive scores of fork selections only offline after all choices. Disclose that this isolates the decision channel conditional on frozen scores rather than the effect of refitting after feedback. Changes to choices, threshold outcomes, and actual gain are reported separately.
- Preflight partition disjointness, selector input boundaries, chronological reveal ordering, replay integrity, fixed profile, legal actions and counts. Only then start the independent arms, which can execute in parallel. Bounded retry applies to service failures and structural validation without changing the declared pool, budget, study profile or conditions. Terminal failures remain in the receipted outputs. No replacement seeds.
- Verdicts: G1 considers the paired above-WT AP difference versus the validation-selected baseline for each budget and its interval, with rank/tail performance as checks. G2 uses the conventional acquisition primary endpoint and WT hits. G3 requires genuine proposed actions, precommitted empirical hypothesis, real revealed measurement, and a subsequent request containing evaluable feedback, even if the hypothesis failed. G4 requires actual paired improvement over strong non-LLM methods, not model explanation or novelty. G5 requires a locally verified clean-clone/API setup and observation import/continue workflow, and reproducible offline evidence. Positive point differences without persuasive uncertainty or robustness support yield MODIFY rather than a general superiority claim.
