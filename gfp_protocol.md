# GFP fluorescence evolution protocol

## Research question

With a fixed assay budget, does an LLM improve discovery of brighter GFP variants compared with random sampling or direct regression ranking? Does an observed-only biochemical knowledge panel change which candidates are selected and improve measured discovery? The unit of evaluation is a GFP variant in the pinned Sarkisyan fluorescence archive. The experiment concerns fluorescence of the reference GFP scaffold.

## Fixed controls and endpoints

- Use the authenticated public ProteinGym GFP_AEQVI_Sarkisyan_2016 catalog. Validate the exact bytes, mutation syntax, residue identity, sequence reconstruction and uniqueness before fitting. Record the assay's score scale, direction, wild-type anchor and publication in the dataset source manifest.
- The model inputs are fixed GFP amino-acid substitutions. Fold data by stable IDs and keep 96 initial observations (including independently measured WT), 120 model-validation entries, 384 replay candidates and 120 final prediction holdout entries disjoint within each seed. Mutations are capped at four substitutions relative to the fixed GFP reference. The hidden measurement catalog is read by the evaluation side of the study. The candidates passed to selectors contain sequence, mutations and predicted score but no unobserved archive labels.
- Formal study uses three paired seed blocks, 17, 29 and 43, with three rounds and five distinct measurements per arm per round. Use seed 7 and one round for interface smoke. Keep the formal splits and decision budgets fixed.
- The main comparison includes uniform random sampling, direct prediction ranking, DeepSeek with knowledge OFF and DeepSeek with knowledge ON. Claude Sonnet, GPT and local Qwen provide additional OFF/ON cells under the same schedule. The two direct controls appear once per seed and are reused as the matched controls.
- The per-arm forest is fit exclusively on its observations. Mutations are represented in a position-aware encoding. Retain Ridge as a competing held-out prediction benchmark. The standalone comparison measures precision and recall at five against measured WT; formal held-out diagnostics use rank, MSE and precision and recall at ten against the evaluation split's upper decile. Refit after each batch, and retain a final holdout for external prediction diagnostics. No validation or holdout labels appear in decision messages or feedback tools.
- The LLM selects from a 48-item menu. It contains 24 leaders of the current model and 24 seeded lower-ranked members of the same eligible pool. The admissible pool and experiment budget are shared. Check the chosen batch against the arm's own contemporaneous RF top five in addition to the independent direct-ranking arm.
- Record concrete substitutions and reconstruct each corresponding full GFP sequence. The LLM must explicitly substantiate the proposed substitution set, then call the score tool and return a validated ordered batch. Knowledge ON adds fixed biochemical descriptors and summaries derived from that arm's observations. Both arms retain the same output contract, predictor and scoring checks.
- The primary endpoint is the best archived fluorescence score found, expressed as improvement over the best initial observation after 15 measurements. Secondary outcomes include above-reference hits and the rank of the first hit, greedy overlap, seeded-menu uptake, candidate novelty, forecast errors and failure examples. Compare each pair by seed. Interpretation is descriptive for the three archival partitions.
- An observation is a catalog measurement queried only after a valid batch, or a value explicitly imported as a measurement. A proposal outside the measured catalog remains prediction-only until a new measurement is imported. The workflow does not run physical experiments.
- End each arm after its fixed budget. If a provider or structural validation fails, preserve the trace and use the same frozen policy for a bounded retry or verified replay of successful earlier responses. Report missing cells if recovery fails. Do not replace a model based on outcomes.

## Feedback audit

Reconstruct archive trajectories for all model cells. Use DeepSeek seed 17 and 29 at the decision after round 1, for knowledge OFF and ON, chosen before formal outcomes. Use the original shared initial observations to fit a fixed predictor. Freeze the eligible catalog and decision menu, exclude the first-round queried IDs in both conditions, and present the first-round measured results only to With Feedback. Use each backend's same configuration and acquisition budget. Compare both conditions with the shared fixed model top five. Score accepted batches after decision using recorded assay values. Report selection overlap, newly cited evidence, the best and mean measured outcomes, regret to the observed oracle maximum on the same menu and relative performance to direct ranking. Evidence availability, a change of action, and an improvement in measured discovery are distinct. A free-text hypothesis requires an explicit, predeclared falsifiable criterion to be treated as tested.
